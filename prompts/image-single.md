```text
Create ONE square illustration in modern flat vector style with bright, clean colours, matching a set of simple educational pictures.
Use a very light pastel background. One clear main subject, centred, easy to recognise on a small phone screen.
The picture illustrates the English word "{{word}}", but the word itself must NOT appear.
Scene: {{hint}}
Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, signs, logos, user-interface elements, icons or typographic symbols anywhere. Screens, papers and book covers must be blank or show simple shapes only.
```
